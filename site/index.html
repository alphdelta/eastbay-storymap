Shattered Circles

A Spatial Equity Audit of East Bay Transit

Life Along the Corridors

Freeways and rail lines don't just move people through the East Bay—they divide the communities around them. This map follows AC Transit routes that parallel major corridors. Each bus stop becomes a lens: a half-mile walking polygon reveals how much of the surrounding neighborhood is actually reachable on foot.

Press Next to begin the guided walkthrough, or toggle polygons in the Lines tab to explore on your own.
